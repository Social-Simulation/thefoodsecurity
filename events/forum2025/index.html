2025 Global Food Security Forum for Young Scientists

The 2025 Global Food Security Forum for Young Scientists is organized by Huazhong Agricultural University, the Global Food Security Association for Young Scientists, and the Innovation and Talent Introduction Base for Health Economics and Agricultural Green Development, and co-organized by the Laboratory for Intelligent Food Security Governance and the Digital Agricultural Research Institute, Huazhong Agricultural University.
